Relationships:
- A **User** can make multiple **Bookings**
- A **RegisteredUser** has a **travelHistory** of **Bookings**
- A **Booking** is linked to one **Bus**
- A **Booking** reserves one **Seat**
- A **Booking** results in one **Payment**
- A **User** can submit multiple **IncidentReports**
- A **User** can submit multiple **Feedback** entries
- An **Admin** can manage multiple **Buses**
- An **Admin** can resolve multiple **IncidentReports**
- A **Bus** includes multiple **Seats**
